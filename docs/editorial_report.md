# Editorial report

The Question That Remains is presently a complete 8:15 preproduction animatic, with a 90-second trailer and a 60-second vertical question sequence. It moves from public achievement toward service and ends with a question attributed to Jesus in Mark 8:29a.

It does not claim that every candidate converted, that fame establishes theological truth, or that testimony settles another person’s doubt. Proposed ethical reflection is kept distinct from explicit belief. Stephen Fry’s broader challenge about suffering is reserved as an objection without an invented answer. King’s planned service sequence must retain the sermon’s critique of status and exclusion.

Every archival slot is a visible reservation. No historical footage, archival audio, portraits, copied music or synthetic voices appear. The only direct quotation rendered is the verified public-domain biblical question. The film uses original geometric design and intentional silence. On-screen editorial questions are the production’s own text.

Research established several official pages and a transcript, not cleared audiovisual excerpts. Publication remains blocked by missing masters, timecodes, context verification, licenses and human approval. The build is useful for evaluating narrative, pace and typography; it is not a finished archival documentary.
